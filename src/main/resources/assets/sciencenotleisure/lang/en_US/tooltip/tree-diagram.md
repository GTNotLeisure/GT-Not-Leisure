Each recipe starts from 64 circuits, using recipes from the Nano Circuit Factory, Circuit Assembly Line and Circuit Assembler.
Consumable items and fluids cost 90%, rounded down. Components are discounted before wrapping; leftovers stay loose.
Submit the required mainframes in the unlock panel before crafting a family. Partial payments are saved.
Only the highest mainframe in a family counts. For example, 52,488 UV Crystal Mainframes unlock all four crystal circuits, IV through UV.
Independent circuits use their own tier: ULV costs 524,288. Each next tier costs 25% less than the previous one, rounded down.
Unlocked circuits still need the nanite tier shown in NEI. Nanites range from T1 to T10.
There is one main nanite slot and three support slots. The main nanite sets efficiency, allowed tier, loss and bonus chances.
Supports add parallels only. In order of capacity, they contribute 50%, 25% and 12.5%. Under-tier or unneeded supports do not participate.
Participating nanites stay locked until the whole batch is delivered. The nanite items are not consumed.
The three maintenance fluids are Grade 4, Grade 6 and Grade 8 Purified Water.
T1–T3 use the first fluid. T4–T6 use the first two together. T7–T10 use all three together. Higher tiers need more fluid.
The main nanite and active supports each need maintenance. Faster work uses more fluid; the runtime panel shows the current rates.
Stable nanites have no loss with full maintenance. Gambling nanites retain at least 22%, 14% or 6% loss chance in the three stages.
A loss event reduces that parallel's output by 25%. An ordinary bonus event adds 30%. Both events can happen together.
Extra high-grade fluid cannot remove a low-tier gambling nanite's risk. Extra computation does not reduce risk either.
Wired and wireless computation input hatches are supported. Required computation depends on the recipe, actual parallels and base processing speed.
Computation updates during a batch. Half the required supply gives one quarter of the speed and 1.75 times the EU/t.
More computation speeds up work. Beyond twice the requirement, each further increase gives a smaller speed gain.
With no computation, work continues at 1/64 speed and four times the EU/t.
Missing power, maintenance fluid, structure or output space pauses work. Paid inputs, progress and the batch result are saved.
Idle and paused machines use no new maintenance fluid. Work resumes when supplies return. Removing the controller preserves unlocks and its stored items.
Further nanite balancing is planned. Nanites marked as having no registered profile cannot participate yet.
